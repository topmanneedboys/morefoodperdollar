ValuePilot offline identity catalog

This catalog contains a bounded selection of product identity and display
metadata derived from Open Food Facts. Open Food Facts data is provided by its
community and is available under the Open Database License (ODbL-1.0) and the
Database Contents License. See:

https://openfoodfacts.github.io/openfoodfacts-server/api/
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

This asset is identity-only. It does not assert a retailer, store, price,
promotion, package quantity, stock state, availability, or freshness. The
Canada-labelled selection is a discovery baseline for the GTA and Metro
Vancouver surfaces, not proof that a product is stocked in either region.
