## Advertiser agreement conclusions

The advertiser agreement reviewed with the Offer is materially compatible with a controlled ValuePilot validation application:

- no blanket anti-comparison restriction was found
- no blanket mobile/software prohibition conflicts with the authenticated DSA profile flag
- digital properties providing a user-requested benefit are contemplated
- automatic Offer assignment exists, with a seven-day review period before deemed acceptance, so notices must be monitored
- privacy, cookie-consent, tracking, security and breach-notification obligations are substantial and must be satisfied before any production tracking use
- AI/automated promotional content is contemplated only if accurate, reviewed and compliant
- false, misleading or unsupported product claims are prohibited
- the general IP/content license is narrow and referral-oriented
- the general agreement does not establish blanket rights to persist, cache, index, republish or redistribute the full Product Catalog/product feed
- advertiser approval and stated product-feed access must still be followed by inspection of actual feed-specific terms and permissions
