How it works

From product evidence to a useful comparison.

01

Discover

Search for products and relevant offers from authorized shopping-data sources as coverage becomes available.

02

Normalize

ValuePilot interprets money, package quantities, units, availability, and promotion evidence in a consistent way.

03

Compare

Deterministic ranking highlights strong-value options while keeping uncertain or stale evidence from silently winning.

Built around trust

Useful recommendations should explain themselves.

ValuePilot is designed to preserve where shopping evidence came from, distinguish verified evidence from weaker signals, and keep commercial incentives separate from value ranking.

Evidence-aware

Source, freshness, availability, and promotion provenance are treated as part of the shopping evidence.

Commission-independent ranking

Affiliate commission rates, advertiser payouts, or sponsorship do not determine which product receives the strongest value recommendation.

Truthful uncertainty

Unverified, stale, or incomplete information can be shown as context without being allowed to silently influence a Best Value decision.

Current status

Pre-launch and provider-validation stage.

ValuePilot's deterministic comparison foundation and consumer search workflow are under active development. The project is currently evaluating authorized real-world shopping-data providers before introducing a production network data boundary.

No claim is made that the public development build provides live retailer prices, inventory, or promotions today.

Retail and data partnerships

Building a trustworthy product-discovery layer.

ValuePilot is interested in authorized product, offer, price, availability, and promotion data that can support transparent consumer comparison experiences.
